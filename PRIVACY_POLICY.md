# Mileage Trips Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 26, 2026

Mileage Trips is designed to create and manage mileage trip sheets on the user's device.

## Data handled by the app

The app may store the following information when the user enters or selects it:

- Company name, address, phone number, fax number, and company logo
- Driver, truck, route, mileage, fuel, and receipt information
- Photos selected by the user for company logos and fuel receipts

## How data is used

This information is used only to create, save, display, print, and share trip sheets and PDF reports requested by the user.

## Storage and sharing

The current version stores app information locally on the device. Mileage Trips does not operate an advertising network, sell personal information, or transmit app data to a developer-controlled server.

Information leaves the app only when the user chooses a system sharing or printing action, such as sharing a generated PDF. The selected destination's own privacy policy then applies.

## Photo access

Photo-library access is requested only when the user chooses to attach a company logo or receipt image. The app does not request microphone access.

## Data deletion

Users can delete individual trip records inside the app. Removing the app from the device also removes its locally stored app data, subject to the device platform's backup and restore behavior.

## Children's privacy

Mileage Trips is a business productivity tool and is not directed to children under 13.

## Changes

This policy may be updated when app features or data practices change. The latest version will be published in this repository.
